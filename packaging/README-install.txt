Buds Sidekick

1. Install Ollama first (https://ollama.com/download) and open it once.
2. Drag "Buds Sidekick" to Applications.
3. The app is not signed by Apple, so macOS warns the first time. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click "Open Anyway"
   next to "Buds Sidekick was blocked", enter your password, then Open. (Older macOS: right-click the app, Open, Open.)
4. The first launch sets itself up (a few minutes, needs internet), then opens Sidekick in its own window. Inside the app, Setup offers "Download what is missing" for the language models (a few GB).
   Practice mode needs no earbuds; it is used automatically when no Galaxy Buds is connected. For real buds: switch Bluetooth off on your phone and connect them to this Mac first.

Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded. Your data: ~/Library/Application Support/Buds Sidekick

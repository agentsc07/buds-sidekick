Buds Sidekick

1. Install Ollama first (https://ollama.com/download) and open it once.
2. Drag "Buds Sidekick" to Applications.
3. The app is not signed by Apple. The first time: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
4. A Terminal window installs everything (a few minutes the first time, models are a few GB), then your browser opens the dashboard.
   Practice mode needs no earbuds. For real buds: switch Bluetooth off on your phone and connect them to this Mac first.

Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded. Your data: ~/Library/Application Support/Buds Sidekick

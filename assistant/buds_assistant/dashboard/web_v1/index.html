English ⇄ Telugu

Telugu speech recognition uses a Telugu-tuned Whisper: correct on clean synthetic speech (the only test so far), unverified on real voices, and its transcripts are never stored. Turn on telugu stt and interpreter in Settings to hear English whispered for Telugu speech. Say "translate to Telugu: …" or use the boxes below.

Settings

Privacy

  • Everything runs on this Mac: speech recognition, speaker identity, memory, language models. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The replay buffer lives in RAM only and is wiped by Private mode and on exit.
  • Unknown voices are never stored. You can name them during the conversation, which stores what they said.
  • Forgetting a person deletes their voiceprint, transcripts, audio, facts and hints.
  • Recording or identifying people without consent is illegal in many places. Enrolment requires a consent note.
